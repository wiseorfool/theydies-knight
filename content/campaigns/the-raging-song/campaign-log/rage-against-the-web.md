## Summary

The Raging Song cornered Nezznar the Spider within the cavernous halls of Wave Echo Cave. The drow mastermind stood flanked by bugbear brutes near a towering dwarven statue. At the feet of the monument lay the bound, motionless form of Nundro Rockseeker. Anticipating combat, Aeolus smothered the center of the hall beneath a dome of magical silence. Combat sparked the moment Jae Paire strode into the chamber, summoning radiant spirits that carved into the surrounding shadows.

Nezznar slipped past the silence field and retaliated with dark conjuration, summoning an imposing fiend from across the planes. Alinar reacted without hesitation. He slipped through space with a teleportation spell, lined up his sight, and struck the drow with crackling eldritch beams. The blast hurled Nezznar back into the silence field. The blow shattered Nezznar's concentration, snapping the portal shut and banishing the yugoloth. Aeolus seized the momentum. He cast an incendiary spell to turn Nezznar's spider-topped staff searing hot, forcing the villain to drop the rod. Aeolus flashed forward, snatched the weapon from the floor, and felt phantom arachnids scuttle across his flesh.

The adventurers broke the enemy with overwhelming control. Melody warped the battlefield with a potent song of affliction, cursing Nezznar and his guards with doubt while her supernatural glamour sent a bugbear fleeing in terror. Alinar unleashed his celestial shroud, wreathed himself in necrotic terror, and froze Nezznar in place with paralyzing magic. Aeolus battered the defenseless mage with an oceanic tempest, slamming him against the stone walls. With the boss pinned, Jae Paire stepped forward and delivered a club strike to Nezznar's temple, knocking the drow cold. Melody and Alinar eliminated the remaining bugbear guards with precise blasts and shattered thunder.

With the chamber cleared, the party bound the fallen drow in chains. Aeolus channeled healing magic into Nundro, rousing the grieving dwarf who learned of his brother's demise at the cave entrance. Jae Paire used smelling salts to shock Nezznar back to consciousness. Under intense interrogation and crude threats from Alinar, Nezznar confessed his ties to the Zhentarim. He revealed his ambition to use the Forge of Souls to piece together shattered Netherese obelisk shards scattered near Phandalin. When Nezznar spat defiance, Nundro stepped forward, invoked the name of the dwarven god Dumathoin, and drove a mining pick straight into the villain's skull. The victory brought peril of its own: Aeolus found his fingers fused to the spider staff, and when Alinar examined the artifact, Lolth struck his mind with a vicious curse. Jae Paire called upon Ilmater to sever Lolth's grasp on Alinar, leaving Aeolus to endure the cursed grip until morning light.

## Threads

- **The Netherese Obelisk Shards:** Nezznar revealed that pieces of an ancient Netherese obelisk exist around and beneath Phandalin. The party must investigate local landmarks to locate these artifacts before other factions claim them.
- **Zhentarim Involvement:** Nezznar secured financing from the Black Network to find the Forge of Souls. The Zhentarim may send agents to inspect Wave Echo Cave when their contact fails to report.
- **The Spider's Curse:** Aeolus remains unable to let go of Nezznar's staff. Jae Paire must expend divine power at dawn to cleanse the druid of Lolth's lingering grip.
- **Reclaiming Wave Echo Cave:** Nundro Rockseeker survived his capture. The party must escort him to Phandalin and help secure the mines and the Forge of Souls for the surviving Rockseeker family.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Wrath in the Temple

#### *The Raging Song breaches the inner sanctum to confront Nezznar the Spider and his retinue.*

- Jae Paire wins initiative and charges forward, summoning a maelstrom of *Spirit Guardians* that scorches both Nezznar and a bugbear.
- Nezznar retreats from the magical silence bubble and casts *Summon Fiend*, calling an imposing yugoloth onto the field.
- Alinar teleports via *Misty Step* and lands an *Eldritch Blast* on Nezznar, knocking him back into the silence bubble and breaking his concentration on the fiend.
- The yugoloth gets sucked into an interplanar rift that appears alien and non-fiendish.
- Aeolus casts *Heat Metal* on Nezznar's staff, forcing the villain to drop the weapon. Aeolus teleports in, snatches the staff with a natural 20 Sleight of Hand check, and feels phantom spiders crawling across his skin.
- Melody activates Mantle of Majesty and casts *Bane*, debilitating Nezznar and a bugbear guard.
- Nezznar retaliates with *Magic Missile* against Jae Paire, breaking concentration on *Spirit Guardians*.
- Alinar activates Necrotic Shroud to frighten Nezznar, then uses an Ioun stone spell and a Lucky point to paralyze him with *Hold Person*.
- Aeolus unleashes Wrath of the Sea, shoving the paralyzed drow into the wall with tidal frost.
- Jae Paire charges in with a non-lethal cudgel blow, knocking Nezznar unconscious.
- Melody commands one bugbear to flee with a bonus action *Command*, then vaporizes the straggler with *Shatter* while Alinar guns down the second guard with *Eldritch Blast*.

### Scene 2: The Miner's Justice

#### *An interrogation reveals dark plots before vengeance claims the Spider.*

- Aeolus casts *Cure Wounds* through the Staff of Healing on Nundro Rockseeker, who learns of his brother's death and demands blood.
- Jae Paire revives Nezznar with smelling salts following a successful DC 22 Medicine check.
- Alinar threatens Nezznar with a soiled bugbear loincloth to force compliance.
- Nezznar confesses under interrogation: he partnered with the Zhentarim to find the Forge of Souls so he could assemble shards of a shattered Netherese obelisk.
- Nezznar hints that additional obelisk shards sit right beneath Phandalin.
- Nezznar attempts a psychic *Suggestion* on Alinar ("Choke on it yourself"), but Alinar resists the mental intrusion.
- Nundro steps forward, denounces the drow before a statue of Dumathoin, and executes Nezznar with a mining pick.
- The adventurers loot 3,408 gold pieces and a Potion of Vitality from the chamber.

### Scene 3: The Weaver's Bite

#### *A cursed artifact strikes back at the victors.*

- Aeolus discovers he cannot open his fingers or drop Nezznar's staff.
- Alinar casts *Identify* on the staff to inspect the enchantment.
- A psychic vision of a drow woman astride a monstrous spider invades Alinar's consciousness as Lolth lashes out.
- Alinar fails the saving throw against Lolth's curse, suffering a reduction of 27 to his maximum hit points.
- Jae Paire invokes the power of Ilmater through his Wind Beads, casting *Greater Restoration* to purge the curse from Alinar.
- With his high-tier magic spent for the day, Jae Paire promises to cleanse the staff's hold on Aeolus once the sun rises.

## NPCs

### Nezznar the Spider

A male drow mage and Zhentarim-backed explorer who seized Wave Echo Cave to find the Forge of Souls. He sought the forge to repair shattered Netherese obelisks. Paralyzed, stripped of his staff, and interrogated by the party, he met his end when Nundro drove a mining pick into his head.

### Nundro Rockseeker

A dwarven explorer and brother to Gundren and Tharden. The party found him bound and unconscious inside the temple hall. Upon learning of Tharden's murder, Nundro took revenge by executing the helpless Nezznar.

## Locations

### Wave Echo Cave

A subterranean cavern complex holding the legendary Forge of Souls. The party navigated its deep chambers to locate Nezznar's final redoubt beside an ancient shrine dedicated to Dumathoin.

## Items

### Spider Staff

A magical quarterstaff made of dark metal topped with a sculpted spider emblem. Nezznar dropped the staff under the heat of Aeolus's spell. The artifact bears a potent curse from Lolth, binding itself to Aeolus's grip and punishing Alinar's mental probe.

### Wind Beads

A magical item held by Jae Paire. He expended a daily charge to cast *Greater Restoration*, severing Lolth's parasitic curse from Alinar.

### Potion of Vitality

A very rare magical draught discovered among Nezznar's personal hoard alongside 3,408 gold pieces.

## Spells

### Heat Metal

Cast by Aeolus on Nezznar's spider staff. The spell dealt 10 fire damage and forced the drow mage to drop his primary focus.

### Hold Person

Cast by Alinar via an Ioun Stone. Reinforced with the Lucky feat, the spell paralyzed Nezznar and left him defenseless against incoming strikes.

### Greater Restoration

Channeled by Jae Paire through his Wind Beads. The spell cured Alinar of Lolth's curse and restored his reduced hit point maximum.

## Resources

### Story Points

Heroic narrative points used during the encounter. Jae Paire burned a story point to secure a non-lethal strike against Nezznar after a near miss.

### Necrotic Shroud

A celestial racial transformation invoked by Alinar. The shroud caused surrounding foes to tremble in fear, enabling Alinar to lock down Nezznar.

### Mantle of Majesty

A Glamour Bard feature activated by Melody. The feature granted her an unearthly visage and enabled her to cast *Command* as a bonus action without expending spell slots.

## Boons

### Level 7 Milestone

Upon destroying Nezznar, liberating Nundro Rockseeker, and securing the Forge of Souls, GM Jo Lee awarded the party a milestone level advancement to Level 7.
